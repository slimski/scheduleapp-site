Never miss a game again.

Every football club you follow on one screen: the next match, the last result, and no spoilers unless you ask for them.

The next match, at a glance

Follow every club you care about

Pick clubs from England, France, Germany, Italy, Spain, Russia and Ukraine. Upcoming, in-progress and finished matches for all of them, in one list.

Match details

Tap any match for the kick-off time and countdown, stadium, referee and competition. Before a game, compare both clubs' recent results and look back at their previous meetings.

No spoilers

Scores are hidden by default, so a match you're saving for later stays a surprise. Reveal a single match when you're ready, or switch scores on for good.

Never miss kick-off

Turn on reminders to get a notification shortly before each of your clubs' matches.

Widgets for Home Screen and Lock Screen

See your next match at a glance, without opening the app.

Works offline

The last schedule you loaded stays on screen when you lose your connection.

Private by default

No account, no ads. Don't Miss Next Game does not collect any personal information or track your activity. Read the privacy policy.
